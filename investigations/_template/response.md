# Response

## Containment

- Review the consenting application.
- Revoke the consent grant if it is not sanctioned.
- Review the assumed role for unauthorized use.

## Eradication

- Remove the unauthorized application or consent.
- Rotate exposed credentials.

## Recovery

- Restore normal access after validation.

## Verification

- Confirm the consent is removed.
- Confirm no further role assumptions from the same principal.
